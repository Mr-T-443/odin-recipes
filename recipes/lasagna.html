Classic Lasagna

Description

This classic lasagna features comforting, rich layers of savory meat sauce, tender pasta sheets, and a creamy mixture of melted cheeses. It is a traditional favorite perfect for feeding a family or hosting a small gathering.

The key to its incredible flavor is letting the seasoned tomato and beef sauce simmer gently before assembly. This allows the garlic and herbs to thoroughly infuse into the dish.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna sheets
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 3 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Brown the ground beef with onion and garlic in a pan. Stir in the crushed tomatoes and let the sauce simmer for 15 minutes.
  2. Cook the lasagna sheets in boiling water according to the package instructions, then drain them.
  3. Layer a small amount of sauce in the bottom of a baking dish, followed by a layer of noodles, a spread of ricotta, and a handful of mozzarella.
  4. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used, ensuring the top layer is completely covered in mozzarella.
  5. Bake in a preheated oven at 375°F (190°C) for about 45 minutes until the cheese is bubbling and golden.
