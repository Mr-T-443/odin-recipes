Margherita Pizza

Description

The Margherita pizza is a timeless Italian classic characterized by its simple yet excellent combination of fresh ingredients. It represents the colors of the Italian flag with red tomatoes, white mozzarella, and green basil leaves.

The beauty of this dish relies entirely on high-quality basics: a crisp, light crust, a sweet tomato base, and rich, creamy fresh cheese melt.

Ingredients

  • 1 pre-made pizza dough ball
  • 1/2 cup crushed San Marzano tomatoes
  • 4 oz fresh mozzarella cheese, sliced
  • A handful of fresh basil leaves
  • 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • A pinch of sea salt

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to its absolute highest setting, ideally around 500°F (260°C), with a baking sheet or pizza stone inside.
  2. Stretch your pizza dough out on a piece of parchment paper into a round circle.
  3. Spread the crushed tomatoes evenly across the dough, leaving a small border clear around the edges for the crust.
  4. Arrange your fresh mozzarella slices evenly over the tomato sauce and drizzle lightly with extra virgin olive oil.
  5. Bake in the hot oven for 10 to 12 minutes until the outer crust is crisp and dark brown, and the cheese is fully melted. Top with fresh basil right before slicing.
