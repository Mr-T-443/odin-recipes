Chicken Biryani

Description

Chicken Biryani is a world-famous, deeply aromatic rice dish featuring perfectly seasoned chicken, fragrant basmati rice, and a blend of rich spices. It is a celebration of vibrant textures and rich flavors layered together flawlessly.

Each grain of rice stays distinct, picking up the rich golden colors of saffron and spices while absorbing the juices from the marinated meat underneath.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups Basmati rice
  • 1 lb chicken pieces
  • 1 large onion, thinly sliced and fried
  • 1/2 cup yogurt
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tbsp biryani masala powder
  • A pinch of saffron soaked in warm milk
  • Fresh mint and coriander leaves

Steps

  1. Marinate the chicken pieces in yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, biryani masala, and salt for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Parboil the basmati rice in salted water with whole spices until it is roughly 70% cooked, then drain completely.
  3. In a deep heavy-bottomed pot, cook the marinated chicken until tender.
  4. Layer the partially cooked rice directly over the cooked chicken. Top with fried onions, fresh mint, coriander, and the saffron milk.
  5. Cover the pot tightly with a lid and cook on very low heat (dum cooking) for 20 minutes to allow the steam to finish the dish.
